Takeaways (visual read)

Not a survey — a plain-language look at these frames only.

Sonoma County currently lists the Walk-On Beach stair access as closed due to a landslide; bluff walking north from parking may still be possible.

  • These frames show the access cove/bluff over ~1985–2024 for visual comparison only.
  • Note apparent shoreline/bluff changes especially comparing ~2022 vs 2024 (debris apron vs more exposed rock) — tide/season/sensor can also change appearance.
  • In the Oct 2022 tile the cove shows a broader gray debris/beach apron; the May 2024 tile shows a rockier, more stripped-looking shoreline at the same cove.

Not geologic advice; County / geotech is authority on closure and hazard. This is a desk read of satellite/aerial tiles only.
